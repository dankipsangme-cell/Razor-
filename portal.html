Rules & Policies

Transparency is everything. Read these fully before creating your account.

⚠ Risk Disclosure — Read This First

All investment plans on Razor Chart are educational simulation plans. Returns shown (28% daily) represent growth within the platform's simulation engine and are not guaranteed in any financial sense. Do not invest money you cannot afford to lose. Past simulated performance is not indicative of future results. By creating an account you confirm that you understand this is a financial education platform.

💳

Deposit Rules

All deposits must be made via Paystack (M-PESA, card, or bank transfer). Each deposit activates one investment plan. Minimum deposit varies by country. Only one active plan per deposit. Deposits are non-refundable once an investment plan is activated.

📈

Growth & Returns

Investment plans grow at 28% daily on the deposited principal. Growth is credited daily and reflected on your portfolio dashboard. Plans run for a fixed number of days (7–14 depending on plan). Growth is simulated and shown for educational purposes.

💸

Withdrawals

Withdrawals are only available once a plan reaches its target balance. Withdrawal requests are reviewed within 24 hours. Payments are processed via M-PESA to your registered number. Early withdrawals before target are not permitted. Maximum one withdrawal per completed plan.

🤝

Referral Programme

You earn Ksh 100 (or local equivalent) for every friend you refer who makes their first investment. After 5 qualifying referrals, a Ksh 500 bonus unlocks for withdrawal. Self-referral and multi-account creation are strictly prohibited and result in permanent account termination.

🔒

Account Security

Your account is secured with Firebase Authentication and email verification. We will never ask for your password. Enable email verification immediately after registration. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure. Report suspicious activity immediately.

🌍

Eligibility & Jurisdiction

You must be 18 years or older to create an account. The platform is available in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, South Africa, USA, UK, Australia, Ghana and Nigeria. Users are responsible for ensuring compliance with local laws regarding online investment platforms in their jurisdiction.

🚫

Prohibited Activities

The following are strictly prohibited and result in immediate account termination and forfeiture of all balances: creating multiple accounts, submitting fraudulent payment references, attempting to manipulate the referral system, using another person's payment details without consent, or any activity that violates applicable law.

📞

Dispute Resolution

All disputes must be raised via the in-app RazorBot chat or email within 7 days of the transaction in question. Razor Chart reserves the right to request payment verification evidence before processing any dispute. Decisions by Razor Chart management are final.

❓ Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly does my investment activate after payment? ▼
Your investment activates instantly once Paystack confirms your payment. The Paystack reference is automatically verified and your portfolio updates within seconds.
Can I invest from outside Kenya? ▼
Yes. Razor Chart is available in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, South Africa, USA, UK, Australia, Ghana and Nigeria. Plans are shown in your local currency equivalent. Paystack supports payments from all these countries.
What happens if I miss the withdrawal window? ▼
Your completed plan balance stays safely in your account until you request withdrawal. There is no expiry on completed plans. You can withdraw at any time after the target is reached.
How does the referral programme work exactly? ▼
Share your unique referral code from your Account tab. When a friend signs up using your code and makes their first investment, you earn Ksh 100 (or local equivalent). Once 5 friends have invested, your total Ksh 500 bonus unlocks for withdrawal.
Is my payment information stored securely? ▼
Razor Chart does not store any card details. All payments are processed by Paystack, which is PCI-DSS compliant. We only store your Paystack transaction reference as proof of payment. Your financial data never touches our servers directly.
What are the live forex signals? ▼
Forex signals are analyst-generated trade recommendations for currency pairs like EUR/USD, GBP/USD, XAU/USD and more. Each signal includes an entry price, take profit target and stop loss level. They are for educational and informational purposes only and do not constitute financial advice.
Can I have multiple active investment plans? ▼
Yes. You can activate multiple plans simultaneously by making separate deposits. Each plan tracks independently on your portfolio dashboard with its own progress bar, target balance and projected completion date.
